## 3. Charity / “make them feel special” track

### Important (do this right)
- **Make-A-Wish** and big nationals are often slow, formal, and strict about marketing. Don’t promise “we partnered with Make-A-Wish” unless they actually approve.
- Better week-1 path: **local** children’s hospital foundations, church benevolence, pediatric oncology parent groups, Ronald McDonald House (local), school counselors, pastors — people who already know families.
- **Never** use a sick child’s face in ads without a signed media release that clearly allows marketing.
- Lead with gift. Ask permission for story after. If parent says no to marketing, still give the full gift.

### Simple program name
**“Royal Five”** — 5 gifted sessions this launch month.

### What you gift each child
- Session + **Kingdom Chronicles** hardcover ($299 value)  
- Optional: digital share link  
- Keep movie/prints as optional paid later (don’t overcomplicate the gift)

### How to fill 5 seats (this week)
1. Text 5 people you trust (pastors, mom friends, Lachelle network, Brandastic clients who know families in need)  
2. One short note:

> We’re gifting 5 kingdom photo sessions + a storybook to kids who could use a day of feeling brave and loved. If you know a family (illness, hard season, etc.), reply with a parent contact. No cost to them. We only share photos if the parents say yes in writing.

3. You pick 5. Book them on Fri/Sat like any other shoot.  
4. After: ask for testimonial + release if they’re open.

### Marketing use (only with release)
- “We gifted sessions to families in hard seasons” (general)  
- Specific child story only with written OK  
- Ads can say you support families / gift seats without showing a child who didn’t approve
