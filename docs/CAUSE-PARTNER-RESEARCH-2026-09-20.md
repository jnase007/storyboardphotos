# Storybook Photos — Cause Partner Research
Date: 2026-09-20  
Job: Pick the right giveback + gifted-session path from the **business goal**, not random charity names.

---

## 1. Business goal (the filter)

Storybook Photos exists to make a child feel:

- **Special / royal**
- **Brave and loved**
- **The hero of their own story**
- In a keepsake mom will cry over and keep

**Buyer:** moms (and grandmas) with kids ~3–8  
**Offer:** Costa Mesa kingdom session + storybook ($299 / $299 / $499)  
**Brand soul:** dignity, belonging, wonder — Christian-friendly without being a church product  
**Launch need:** trust + bookings Fri/Sat + proof content (with permission)

### Cause must pass this test
1. Does it make a child feel **chosen and special**?  
2. Can moms instantly understand it?  
3. Can we give **money and/or sessions** without a 6-month legal slog?  
4. Can we tell the truth without exploiting kids?  
5. Does it fit Justin/Lachelle’s faith without alienating normal OC moms?

## 2. Cause categories scored against the goal

| Cause type | Fit to “child is royalty / hero” | Mom marketing power | Speed to start | Privacy risk | Faith fit | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Foster / waiting kids (Christian + local)** | Excellent — belonging, family, “you matter” | High with church + many moms | Medium | High if faces used wrong | Excellent | **9.2** |
| **Pediatric illness / wish kids** | Excellent — courage, magic day | Highest name recognition | Slow for official partner | High | Good | **8.5** |
| **Local kids hospital general (CHOC)** | Strong — local kids | Very high in OC | Fast for donations | Medium | Neutral-good | **8.3** |
| **Crisis hosting / Safe Families** | Strong — child safe + loved short-term | Medium (less famous) | Medium | High | Excellent | **8.0** |
| **General “kids charity” vague** | Weak | Low | Fast | Low | Weak | **3.0** |

**Research conclusion:**  
For *this* business, **foster / family-for-kids** is the best strategic match.  
Illness/wish is a close second for emotion, worse for speed and partnership control.

Why foster wins for Storybook:
- Your product is literally **identity + belonging + “this is your story.”** Foster and adoptive families live that wound and that hope.
- Foster kids and foster families often have **almost no beautiful photos** of childhood.
- Christian foster ministries already organize around churches — your natural network.
- You can donate cash publicly and gift sessions through agencies/ministries that already handle consent.
- It doesn’t require pretending you’re Make-A-Wish.

Illness/wish still works as a **secondary** gift lane later — not the core brand cause unless you want that fight.

## 4. Strategic options (ideas built for the business)

### Option 1 — **Recommended primary: “Royal Belonging” foster lane**
**Public money:** $25 from every paid session → **Olive Crest OC** *or* **FosterAll**  
**Gifted product:** monthly/quarterly free kingdom sessions + storybooks for foster/adoptive families or Safe Families host kids (agency-referred)  
**Why this is best for the business**
- Message writes itself: “Every child deserves to feel they belong in the story.”  
- Moms get it. Church moms lean in. Normal OC moms still respect foster care.  
- Your sets/crowns/storybooks are emotionally correct for kids who’ve felt unwanted.  
- Agencies can handle which children are eligible and consent.  
- You’re not competing with Disney-wish logistics.

**Public line:**  
> $25 from every session helps foster and waiting kids in Orange County through [Olive Crest / FosterAll]. We also gift kingdom sessions so kids in hard family seasons can see themselves as brave and loved.

### Option 2 — **Faith-forward variant (if you want church growth engine)**
Same as Option 1, but primary partner = **FosterAll faith network** + 3 OC churches with foster/adopt ministries.  
- Churches announce Royal Five gift seats  
- Foster parents get first booking windows Fri/Sat  
- Paid moms still fund $25 giveback  
**Upside:** distribution + volunteers + warm leads  
**Risk:** looks “churchy” if homepage becomes ministry-coded — keep site mom-universal, church as channel

### Option 3 — **Wish/illness lane (strong emotion, worse ops)**
$25/session to Make-A-Wish OCIE or CHOC + 5 gifted sessions via hospital social work / local foundation.  
**Use if:** Lachelle’s heart is fixed on medical kids.  
**Don’t use as only plan if:** you need partners *this month* for launch.

### Option 4 — **Hybrid (my practical 12-month design)**
| Bucket | Partner | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| Core cash giveback (every booking) | **Olive Crest** (local scale + trust) | $25/session |
| Gifted sessions pipeline | **FosterAll + Safe Families OC + church foster teams** | Royal Five / month |
| Optional third | CHOC or Make-A-Wish | Annual special drive or 1 campaign weekend |

One public name for money. Multiple quiet referral sources for gifts.

### Option 5 — **“Hero Fund” branded internal fund (advanced)**
Create Storybook **Hero Fund**:  
- $25/session goes into tracked fund  
- Quarterly disbursement split e.g. 70% Olive Crest donation + 30% underwrites free sessions  
**Pros:** flexible  
**Cons:** more admin; must still name where money lands or moms get suspicious  

Only do this once volume exists. Launch simpler.

## 5. What NOT to do
- Vague “we support kids in need” with no name  
- “% of proceeds” language  
- Claim Make-A-Wish partnership without approval  
- Put foster kids’ faces in Facebook ads without ironclad guardian/agency releases  
- Make the whole brand “charity studio” so paying moms feel guilty  
- Pick a teen-aging-out org as the *main* face if your hero product is ages 3–8 (Orangewood is good later, wrong mascot now)

## 6. Best approach mechanics (tied to launch)

### A. Money (every paid booking)
- **$25 fixed** per session  
- One named org on site/ads  
- Monthly donate + receipt log  
- Quarterly “we gave $X” note

### B. Gifted sessions (product as ministry)
- 5 seats / month max at launch (capacity)  
- Referral-only (agency/church/Safe Families)  
- Full Chronicles package  
- Release optional; default private  
- Fri/Sat only like paid clients  

### C. Marketing order (moms)
1. Kid as hero / beautiful proof  
2. Transparent $299–$499  
3. Code ROYAL30 (30 days)  
4. Fri/Sat Costa Mesa  
5. One giveback sentence  

### D. Church/Facebook tactic that fits foster angle
- OC mom groups: normal booking ads  
- Church foster/adopt groups: “gift seats for foster families this month” + paid sessions still open  
- Foster parent Facebook groups: careful, value-first, admin permission

## 7. Final recommendation (Tinny call)

### Primary cause for Storybook Photos
**Foster & family-for-kids in Orange County — not Make-A-Wish as the core.**

### Exact lock I’d make today
1. **Cash partner:** **Olive Crest (Orange County)**  
   - Local, known, kids in crisis/foster/family strengthening  
   - Clean public story for all moms  
2. **Gift pipeline:** **FosterAll + Safe Families OC + 2–3 church foster ministries**  
   - Christian alignment without making Olive Crest a “church only” brand  
3. **Amount:** **$25 per paid session**  
4. **Gift brand:** **Royal Five** (5 gifted kingdom sessions / month)  
5. **Illness/wish:** optional Year 1 special (Christmas drive to CHOC or Make-A-Wish) — not the main identity  

### Why this beats Make-A-Wish-as-default
- Closer to your product meaning (belonging + story + family)  
- Better faith fit for you two  
- More workable referral network through churches  
- Still fully respectable to non-church moms  
- You can start gifting through people you already know while donations hit a real OC org  

### If your heart won’t leave medical kids
Then lock **CHOC Foundation** as cash partner and keep foster as gift pipeline — still better than waiting on Make-A-Wish marketing approval.
