## 2. Who we sell to

### Primary buyer
- Moms (and gift-buying dads/grandparents) of kids **ages 3–8**
- Wants a gift that feels magical, personal, and “only for my kid”
- Buys for birthday, Christmas, new baby sibling gift, grandparents → grandkids

### Pain (real words)
- “Every gift feels the same.”
- “I want something they’ll keep, not toss in a week.”
- “I wish they could see themselves as the hero.”
- “I don’t have time to make a custom book myself.”

### Why us (edge)
- Face → hero in a clean coloring-book style (not creepy photoreal)
- Story + optional narration + short movie (most book sites stop at PDF/print)
- Premium “kingdom / royalty” feeling without Disney lawsuit vibes
- Justin can market this nationally (creative + funnels + local partnerships)

## 3. Offer stack (simple)

### Core offer (sell this first)
**Kingdom Quest Storybook**
- Upload photo + name
- Pick 1 of 6 adventures
- Get personalized story pages (print-ready or printed book)
- Target price band to test: **$39 digital / $79–$99 printed** (validate live)

### Order bump
- Bedtime narration audio (ElevenLabs) **+$15–$25**

### Upsell
- 60–90s personalized mini-movie **+$49–$79**

### Later (not week 1)
- Sibling packs, subscription “new quest monthly”, church/school fundraiser kits, grandparent gift flow

### Lead magnet (free, fast to build)
**“Free 1-page hero preview”**  
Parent uploads a kid photo → gets one free royal hero image + short note.  
Email capture required. Soft CTA to full storybook.

Straight line:  
**Content (kid-as-hero demos) → free hero preview → paid storybook → audio → movie**

## 4. Market snapshot (directionally true)

- Personalized children’s books are a growing gift category (roughly high-single to low-double-digit CAGR in industry reports through early 2030s).
- Big names parents already know: Wonderbly, I See Me!, Hooray Heroes, Magic Story, Imagitime-style photo books.
- Gap we can own: **photo likeness + coloring-book charm + read-aloud + short movie**, sold with warm family creative — not generic “AI book” energy.

**Positioning**
Not “another AI generator.”  
**A keepsake studio for parents who want their child to feel chosen and brave.**

## 5. How we grow (channels that fit)

### Free / owned (heavy first)
1. **Short video proof** — before/after: kid photo → hero page → 15s movie teaser  
2. **Parent TikTok / IG Reels / YouTube Shorts** — emotional reaction content  
3. **Email** after free hero preview  
4. **Partner moms** — 10 micro-creators with kids in niche (faith moms, gift moms, homeschool, SoCal moms)

### Paid (small tests only after 10 organic demos)
- Meta ads: lookalike from email buyers + creative = tearjerker grandparents
- Start **$20–$40/day** max until CPA is known

### Local / relationship
- Orange County photographers, boutiques, churches, schools, birthday venues
- “Fundraiser: $10 back to class per book” pilot with 1 school

### Do not waste early budget on
- PR blasts, giant influencer deals, complex SEO content farms, rebuilding the whole stack

**Exit:** first paid orders + written feedback

**Goal:** pick the winning channel and double it

**Exit:** 30-day scorecard + next-month plan

## 9. Risks / red flags
- Face likeness looks weird → parents bounce hard  
- Delivery too slow → gift dates missed  
- Feels “AI cheap” instead of keepsake → price resistance  
- Too many quests/options → decision fatigue  
- Building movie factory before books sell → wasted motion  

**Mitigations**
- Strict art SOP (coloring-book, no photoreal)  
- One hero path, 6 stories max for now  
- Manual-quality review on first 20 orders  
- Sell book first; movie is upsell
