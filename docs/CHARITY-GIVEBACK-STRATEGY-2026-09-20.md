# Storybook Photos — Charity / Giveback Strategy
Date: 2026-09-20  
Question: Who should we give to, and what’s the best way — gifted shoots + “part of each booking goes to charity”?

---

## Tinny’s short answer

**Yes — do a giveback. Keep it local, clear, and honest.**

Best setup for launch:
1. **Gift a few full sessions** to kids who need to feel special (you control who, with pastor/hospital/social-work intros).  
2. **Donate a fixed dollar amount from every paid booking** to **one primary local charity**.  
3. Do **not** lead with “official Make-A-Wish partner” unless they approve you in writing.  
4. Say a **dollar amount per booking**, not a vague “% of proceeds.”

That wins moms’ hearts without legal/brand mess and without eating your whole margin.

## Two different things people mix up

| Model | What it is | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Gifted sessions** | You give free shoot + book to a family | Pure heart; best stories; brand soul | Costs full COGS + time; marketing only with release |
| **B. Portion of each paid booking** | Every mom who pays also funds kids in need | Scales with sales; easy ad line; fair | Must be clear $; must actually send money |
| **C. Official charity partner campaign** | Charity promotes you / logo / joint event | Huge trust | Slow approvals; rules; you may wait months |

**Launch = A + B.**  
**C later** if charity wants you.

## How much to give (math that won’t sink you)

Live packages: **$299 / $299 / $499**

Cash COGS-ish per book session is roughly low hundreds all-in when you count print + time pressure — so don’t pledge 20–30% forever.

### Recommended public promise
**“$25 from every booked session goes to [Charity].”**

Why $25:
- Clear on a $299 booking (~8%)  
- Clear on $499 (~5%)  
- Easy to track: sessions × $25 = donation  
- Big enough to matter; small enough you can keep the lights on  

### Alternatives
| Promise | When to use |
|---|---|
| **$25 per session** | Best default |
| **$50 per Royal Collection only** | If you want to push $499 |
| **$1,000/month minimum** once volume exists | Looks strong; only after books are steady |
| **10% of package price** | OK if you disclose exact % of **price**, not “profits” |

### Avoid saying
- “Portion of proceeds” (vague — regulators and moms both hate this)  
- “Profits” (arguable, messy)  
- “All proceeds” (you’ll go broke)  
- “In partnership with Make-A-Wish” (unless true and approved)

**Better line:**  
> $25 from every Storybook Photos session is donated to [CHOC Foundation / Make-A-Wish Orange County & Inland Empire].

## Legal / honesty basics (plain English)

Not legal advice — practical guardrails:
1. **Actually donate** on a schedule (monthly is fine). Keep a simple log: date, session count, amount, confirmation.  
2. Prefer **fixed $ per unit** over % of profit.  
3. If you use the charity’s **name/logo in ads**, many states treat that as commercial co-venture / cause marketing — get their written OK or stick to “we donate to…” without logo.  
4. Customer payment to **you** is not automatically a tax-deductible gift for them. Don’t tell moms their session fee is a charity receipt unless a proper structure exists (usually it doesn’t on a normal booking).  
5. **Your** business donation may be deductible as a business expense/charitable contribution depending on structure — CPA (RJI) can confirm; don’t invent tax claims in ads.  
6. Kids’ photos: **written media release** or no public story.

## How this helps launch marketing (moms)

Moms buy identity + feeling. The giveback should support the core offer, not replace it.

**Core offer still wins the click:**  
child as hero · beautiful book · transparent price · Fri/Sat · code ROYAL30  

**Giveback is the trust layer:**  
“When you book, a kid in a hard season benefits too.”

### Ad stack order
1. Hero child feeling  
2. Transparent package  
3. First-time code  
4. One line giveback  
5. Book Fri/Sat  

Don’t make the ad only about charity — you’ll get applause, not deposits.

## Make-A-Wish specifically — thoughts

**Good:**
- Instant emotional recognition  
- Local chapter exists (OCIE, Irvine)  
- Perfect mission match with “feel special / brave / loved”

**Hard:**
- They protect the brand carefully  
- Wish kids aren’t a DIY marketing prop  
- Partnership timelines can be slow vs your “book this week” goal  

**Smart Make-A-Wish path:**
1. Start donating $25/session **to** Make-A-Wish OCIE (or CHOC) immediately — no logo needed.  
2. Separately email chapter about **in-kind session gifts** for wish families if useful to them.  
3. Only after written approval: logo, “proud supporter” badge, joint posts.  

Same playbook for CHOC Foundation.
